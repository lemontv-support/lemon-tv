Lemon TV - Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

1. Overview

Lemon TV is an IPTV media player. It does not provide television channels, playlists, or media content. Users add their own playlists.

2. Data Collection

The developer does not collect, store on external servers, sell, or share users' personal information.

3. Playlist Information

Playlist addresses and application settings are stored locally on the user's device and are not transmitted to the developer.

4. Network Connections

The application connects to external servers specified by the user or their IPTV provider to retrieve playlists, program guides, and media streams. These services may process network information according to their own privacy policies.

5. Advertising and Tracking

Lemon TV does not include advertising or user-tracking functionality.

6. Children's Privacy

Lemon TV does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated when necessary. Updates will be published on this page.

8. Contact

For privacy-related questions, please visit the Lemon TV Support page.
